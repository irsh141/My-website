About GMB Expert

We started because local business owners kept getting vague promises. We wanted to do it differently.

Our story

Built by people who have run local campaigns for nearly a decade

GMB Expert began in New York over a decade ago as a two-person team helping restaurants and clinics get more calls from Google. Word spread, and today we support more than 500 businesses across several countries.

Our approach has not changed: understand the business first, fix the fundamentals, measure honestly. We would rather lose a sale than promise a result we cannot control.
